# Customer impact summary — reminder text messages paused

**Author:** Customer support lead (Tomas Reyes)

## Who is affected

Eighty-eight clinics use text-message reminders. Their patients' reminder messages from the past week were among data accessed at our text-message provider. The messages contained the patient's first name, the clinic's name, and the appointment date and time. No surnames, no medical information, no contact details other than the phone number.

## What clinics experience now

- Text reminders are paused from Monday 16:00 until further notice.
- Clinics that have patient email addresses on file are sending email reminders instead, automatically.
- Clinics without patient email addresses currently have no automatic reminders.

## What patients may notice

- No reminder texts for now.
- Possibly, unexpected texts that mention their clinic and an appointment and ask for a payment or to click a link. Those are not from the clinic or from XYZ.

## What clinics can do now

- Check whether reminders are going by email: Settings, then Reminders, shows the active channel.
- Clinics without email reminders can print a daily reminder list from the calendar and call patients.
- Warn patients that the clinic will never ask for payment by text message.
- Each clinic can request the number of its patients affected through support.

## Support script

"Reminder text messages are paused because of a security incident at the provider we use to send them. Your own XYZ account and calendar were not accessed. Reminders will go by email where we have patient emails. Please tell patients the clinic will never ask for payment by text."
