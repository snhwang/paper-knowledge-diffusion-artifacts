# Customer impact summary — Tuesday scheduling incident

**Author:** Customer support lead (Tomas Reyes)

## Who is affected

Fourteen clinic customers are affected. Their appointment lists — patient names, appointment dates and times — were accessible to an unauthorised party for part of Monday and early Tuesday. No other customers are affected. No clinical notes, payment details or contact information were involved.

## What customers experienced

- Clinics using the PDF export feature could not export appointment lists from 03:05 to 12:15 on Tuesday. They received a message that the export was temporarily unavailable.
- No clinic lost access to its calendar, and no appointment data was changed or deleted.
- Some clinics noticed slower page loads on Tuesday morning.

## Workaround during the export outage

Appointment lists could be printed from the calendar view, or downloaded as a spreadsheet from the Reports page, throughout the outage.

## What customers can do now

- Nothing is required to keep using the service. The issue has been fixed.
- The 14 affected clinics will receive a direct call from their account manager on Wednesday and a written notice with details of what was accessed and what we recommend telling their patients.
- Clinics that want a copy of their access log for the period can request it through support.

## Support script

"Yes, we had a security incident on Tuesday that affected the appointment lists of a small number of clinics. It has been fixed. If your clinic is affected, your account manager will contact you directly on Wednesday. Your calendar and appointments were not changed."
