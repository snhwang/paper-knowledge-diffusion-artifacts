# Customer impact summary — unauthorised contact export

**Author:** Customer support lead (Tomas Reyes)

## Who is affected

Staff at 212 customer clinics. Their business contact details — names, work email addresses and work phone numbers — were included in a list exported without authorisation. No patient information and no passwords were involved.

## What customers experienced

Nothing visible in the product. There was no outage and no change to any account.

## What customers may notice

Clinic staff may receive unsolicited emails or calls, possibly presenting as coming from XYZ or from a partner. Any such contact is unofficial.

## What customers can do now

- Treat unexpected contact that refers to their XYZ account with caution, and report it to support so we can track it.
- Remind staff that XYZ never asks for passwords or payment details by email or phone.
- Customers who want confirmation of which of their staff were included can request it through support; we will reply within two business days.

## Support script

"We reviewed a former employee's access and found that a list of customer business contact details had been exported without authorisation. No patient information was involved. You may receive unsolicited contact; please report it to us. We have tightened the controls on exports."
