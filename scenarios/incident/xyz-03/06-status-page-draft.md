# Customer notice — draft for legal review

**Author:** Communications lead (Hannah Whitfield)

## Notice to customers — Monday

On Friday we completed a review of a former employee's access to our systems. We found that a list of customer business contact details — staff names, work email addresses and work phone numbers — had been exported without authorisation shortly before the employee left. No patient information and no account credentials were involved, and our service was not affected.

We have taken steps to prevent this from recurring, including approval and logging for every export of customer data, and we are pursuing the matter through appropriate channels.

You may receive unsolicited emails or calls that refer to your XYZ account. Please treat any such contact as unofficial and report it to our support team.

We will write again if there is anything further you need to know.

## Notes for legal

- We have not named or described the former employee, and we have not given the number of records or the file name.
- "Appropriate channels" is deliberately vague; please confirm it does not imply an accusation.
- Please confirm Monday morning for sending to all customers, and the separate wording for the three customers with contractual notice terms.
